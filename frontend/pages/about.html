About OilSeed Pro

Building India's most trusted digital marketplace for oilseed by-products, empowering farmers and transforming the agricultural value chain.

Our Story

OilSeed Pro was born from a simple observation: despite India being one of the world's largest producers of oilseeds, farmers often fail to capture the full value of their produce, especially by-products like soymeal, oil cakes, and husk.

The 2022 Russia-Ukraine conflict exposed critical vulnerabilities in the oilseed supply chain. While consumer prices surged, farm-gate prices remained stagnant, widening the gap between producers and end consumers.

We set out to bridge this gap by creating a transparent, technology-driven marketplace that connects farmers directly with processors and buyers, eliminating intermediaries and ensuring fair prices for all stakeholders.

Our Mission

To empower every farmer in India to monetize every part of their oilseed harvest by providing direct access to markets, transparent pricing, and secure payment mechanisms.

Our Vision

To become India's largest and most trusted digital platform for oilseed trade, processing 50% of the country's oilseed by-products by 2030 and enabling farmers to increase their income by 40% through better market access.
